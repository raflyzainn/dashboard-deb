15 · Rules of the screen

What is the same on every item

  • Top bar: item name, version chips (click to open an old version, "+ versi baru" to upload), campus numbers on the right (Nilai SK, Batas, Diajukan, n dari 8), a "Riwayat" chip that opens a side sheet.
  • Middle: the file, full width. Word and Excel drawn in the page, scans as image or PDF.
  • Bottom bar: the values to type for this item (saved as you leave the field, per version), at most one or two result chips (green ✓, red !), a "n catatan" chip, and the decision buttons. Nothing else.
  • Decision: the green button is always the "right" answer for that item; the grey one is "wrong" and opens one text box for the note to the campus. A wrong decision sets Perlu revisi; the row turns amber; the campus is told.
  • After a decision the next unfinished item opens by itself. Keyboard: Enter for the green button, Esc back to the dashboard.
  • SK first: every campus list, Tahap 1 and Tahap 2, starts with the SK row, the source of every amount. Tahap 2 shows the same SK line plus Dibayar Tahap 1 and Sisa.
  • Closing rows (Tanda tangan, Lampiran, Pembayaran) sit under the eight items and turn active when all eight are done.
  • Phone: the list becomes a strip of chips above the document, the bar stays at the bottom, inputs stack.
